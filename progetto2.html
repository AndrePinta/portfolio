Designing a new typographic grid to improve the reading experience of long-form essays.

A sandwich embodies history, identity and memory. Whether you find it on a street in Paris, at a market in Mexico or at a stall in Tokyo, it leaves a lasting impression. This book celebrates the pleasure of eating with your hands, without any rules: from sandwiches to bánh mì, from Mexican tortas to Croque Monsieur and Katsu Sando.
